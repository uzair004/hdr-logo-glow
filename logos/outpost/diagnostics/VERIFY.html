1 · Is the effect working on this screen at all?

Flat squares of pure light. If these do not clearly outshine the plain white swatch, the problem is the screen or its settings, not the files.

LinkedIn's default is light mode. The blue tile against white is the harder test — there is less contrast to work with, so the effect reads differently.

If section 1 looks flat

  1. Turn screen brightness down to about 70%. The most common cause by far. At 100% the screen is already at its limit with nothing left in reserve.
  2. Low Power Mode off. It removes the extra range entirely.
  3. Keep the window in front. Background windows lose the extra brightness.
  4. Use Chrome or Safari. Finder icons, Quick Look and column previews flatten it.
  5. Not on an external monitor unless it is a Pro Display XDR.
