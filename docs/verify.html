Display capability

Flat luminance probes. If these do not outshine the plain white swatch, the display or its settings are the limiting factor, not the files.

If the probes look flat

  1. Display brightness at 100% leaves no headroom. Try ~70%.
  2. Low Power Mode caps headroom entirely.
  3. Backgrounded windows have their headroom reclaimed.
  4. External monitors other than Pro Display XDR are SDR.
